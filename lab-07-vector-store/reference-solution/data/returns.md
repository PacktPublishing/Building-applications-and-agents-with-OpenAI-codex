# Northstar Market returns policy

Northstar Market is a fictional business used for this course lab.

Customers may return unopened shelf-stable products within 14 calendar days
of delivery. Proof of purchase is required. Unopened packaged coffee is a
shelf-stable product covered by this policy.

Fresh, chilled, and frozen products are excluded from ordinary returns unless
the goods arrived damaged or the wrong item was delivered. Report damaged or
incorrect goods to support within 24 hours of delivery and include the order
number and a description of the issue.
