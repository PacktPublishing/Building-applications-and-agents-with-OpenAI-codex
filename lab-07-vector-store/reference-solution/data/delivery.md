# Northstar Market delivery policy

Northstar Market is a fictional business used for this course lab.

Next-day delivery orders must be placed before 18:00 Europe/Berlin. Delivery
operates Monday through Saturday. Orders placed after the cutoff are eligible
for the following available delivery day. Northstar does not offer same-day
delivery.

Delivery costs EUR 4.90. Orders with a merchandise total of at least EUR 60
qualify for free delivery.
